Our locations

Location 1: Grand Canyon Leap

Take the leap above the Grand Canyon and feel the rush as you soar over its breathtaking cliffs. It is a heart-pounding adventure with a view you will never forget.

Location 2: Tropical Beach Leap

Take the leap above a tropical beach and feel the rush as you soar over turquoise water and golden sand. It is a heart-pounding adventure in a breathtaking setting.

Location 3: Victoria Falls Leap

Feel the rush of a bungee jump near Victoria Falls, with the mighty Zambezi River and dramatic gorge all around you. It is a thrilling leap framed by one of southern Africas most unforgettable sights.
